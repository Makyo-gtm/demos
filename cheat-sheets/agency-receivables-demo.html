    Build an accounts-receivable / collections tracker for a small web development agency to track which clients owe them money and follow up on overdue payments. This is an internal tool for the agency's own team, not client-facing.

    Two main tables. CLIENTS: Client Name (text), Contact Email (email), Contact Phone (phone). INVOICES: Invoice Number (auto-generated, prefix INV-), a relation to the Client it belongs to, Description (text), Amount (number), Issue Date (date), Due Date (date), Paid (yes/no), Paid Date (date, blank until paid). Add a computed field showing how many days overdue an unpaid invoice is (today minus Due Date), and a computed category grouping that into buckets: Current, 1-30 days overdue, 31-60 days overdue, 61-90 days overdue, and 90+ days overdue. Also add a FOLLOW-UPS table: a relation to the Invoice it's about, Sent Date (auto-set to now), Method (text, e.g. Email or Phone), and a Note (text) — one row every time someone logs that they followed up with a client about an unpaid invoice.

    Screens I need: (1) An overview/dashboard screen showing total amount outstanding, total amount overdue, and how many invoices are currently overdue — show this however you think best represents it. (2) A table of all invoices, filterable by client and by paid/unpaid status, sortable by due date. (3) A view of overdue invoices organized by their aging bucket (Current / 1-30 / 31-60 / 61-90 / 90+ days overdue) so it's easy to see which are the most urgent — again, however you think best represents that grouping. (4) A per-client screen showing that client's invoices and how much they owe in total. (5) A button on an overdue invoice to log that a payment reminder was sent, which records a new row in Follow-Ups with today's date.
